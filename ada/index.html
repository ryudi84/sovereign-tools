What Our Scanner Checks

Critical Issues

Invalid ARIA attributes, missing form labels, content hidden from screen readers, focusable elements with no accessible name, keyboard traps.

Serious Issues

Color contrast below 4.5:1 ratio, missing link text, images without alt text, auto-playing media, missing document language.

Moderate Issues

Heading hierarchy violations, missing landmark regions, redundant ARIA roles, tab order issues, missing skip navigation.

Best Practices

Proper semantic HTML, responsive design, focus indicators, error identification, consistent navigation patterns.

Methodology & Transparency

We believe in full transparency about what automated scanning can and cannot do.

  • Engine: axe-core (Deque Systems) — the industry-standard open-source accessibility testing engine used by Microsoft, Google, and the US government.
  • Standard: WCAG 2.1 Level A and AA success criteria, as specified in the DOJ's ADA Title II final rule.
  • Scope: 101 US cities with populations over 200,000 — homepage only. Full-site scans typically reveal 3-5x more violations.
  • Scan date: March 8, 2026.
  • Limitation: Automated testing covers approximately 57% of WCAG 2.1 AA success criteria. The remaining 43% requires manual expert review (e.g., reading order, cognitive load, plain language).
  • Not legal advice: This is a technical readiness assessment. Consult legal counsel for compliance certification.
